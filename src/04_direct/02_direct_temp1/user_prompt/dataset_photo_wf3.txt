Task:  
Predict the photodegradation of the polymer-blend film under the following experimental setup.

Overview of the Experiment:  
- The photodegradation of polymer blend films (thickness: 70–80 nm) used in organic solar cells was measured.  
- Four components (WF3, P3HT, PCBM, oIDTBR) were mixed in fractions that sum to 1.  
- Samples were exposed to light from a metal-halide lamp at 1 sun intensity under ambient conditions for 18 hours.  
- The photodegradation was quantified by the integrated absorbance loss from 300–800 nm (lower loss = higher stability).
